Scenarios are simulated. Scores describe automation-like behavior and are not proof that anyone is a bot, a fraudster or a scammer. The ML model is an Isolation Forest trained on simulated human sessions; its "unusual inputs" show how a session differs from that training data and are not a causal explanation of the score. When the FSM score is 0, the hybrid score can be at most 0.40 × ML.
